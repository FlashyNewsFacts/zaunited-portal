# Code of Conduct

The Kingdom of Zaun welcomes everyone. ZAUNITED's audience includes young
people, so our community spaces must be safe, friendly and respectful.

## Our standards

Please:
- Be kind, patient and welcoming, especially to newcomers.
- Give constructive feedback and accept it gracefully.
- Keep content suitable for a general, all-ages audience.

Not acceptable:
- Harassment, insults, bullying or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual, violent or otherwise inappropriate content.
- Sharing anyone's personal information without permission.
- Spam, self-promotion or deliberately disruptive behaviour.

## Scope

This applies to the repository, issues, discussions, pull requests and any
community feature of the Kingdom Portal.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content and restrict or ban participants who break
these rules. Report problems privately to the maintainers using
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/FlashyNewsFacts/zaunited-portal/security/advisories/new)
or a contact address that will be published here before the repository opens
to the public.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
